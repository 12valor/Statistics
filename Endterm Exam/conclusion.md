# Website Bounce Rate Analysis - Conclusion

A 1-Sample T-Test was performed on the Bounce Rate to compare it against a standard of 0.50. Outliers were removed via z-score and the data was normalized using the Central Limit Theorem.

- **Hypothesized Mean:** 0.50
- **Sample Mean:** ~0.278
- **T-Statistic:** -85.5268
- **P-Value:** 0.0000

With a p-value of 0.0000 (well below $\alpha = 0.05$), the null hypothesis is rejected. The website's Bounce Rate is significantly lower than the 0.50 standard.

- **Cohen's d:** 8.55 (Large effect size)
- **Power:** 100%

The difference is highly significant and practically meaningful.
